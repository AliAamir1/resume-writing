## Remote constraints (HARD requirements)

- **Location:** based in Lahore, Pakistan (PKT, UTC+5). Will NOT relocate.
- **Work model:** globally-remote roles ONLY. Job must allow employment from Pakistan as a remote contributor.
- **DEAL-BREAKERS (must NOT apply):**
