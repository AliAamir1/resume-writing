## Form-fill rule

The Easy Apply work-history slots are pre-filled from LinkedIn for entries with `defaultValue` set. For ghost slots that LinkedIn leaves blank, **do not fabricate**. Skip those slots and the form usually still submits. If submit fails with HTTP 400 due to required-but-blank slot, mark the job as `skipped_unknown_q` and queue for manual review.
