# TSKR

Freelancer marketplace with separate client and freelancer dashboards for posting, browsing, and managing jobs and contracts.

## Bullets

- *Two-Sided Marketplace*: Built a freelancer marketplace with distinct client and freelancer dashboards covering job posting, browsing, applications, and contract management across multiple categories.
- *Real-Time Updates*: Implemented live job and contract state updates over Socket.IO.
- *Full-Stack Development*: Built on Next.js with MongoDB persistence.
