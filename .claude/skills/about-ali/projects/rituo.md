## Product and Launch

- **Full-Stack Product Ownership:** Built and shipped an AI wellness product end to end: 140-screen mobile app, 97-endpoint API, cloud infrastructure.
- **Shipped Paid Subscriptions:** Launched on the App Store and Google Play with server-side receipt verification, store webhooks and restore flows.

## AI and Voice

- **Halved Per-User AI Cost:** Cut monthly AI spend per user from $30 to $16 by migrating speech and inference vendors.
- **Real-Time AI Voice Coach:** Built an AI coach that generates and streams spoken guidance to users live during each session.
- **Voice-Driven Journaling:** Built live speech-to-text over a streaming socket so users journal by speaking instead of typing.
- **AI Personalisation Engine:** Generated personalised daily routines from each user's mood, goals and history using GPT-5 structured outputs.
- **Generative Audio Pipeline:** Converted AI-written scripts into continuous narrated audio tracks using speech synthesis and automated ffmpeg stitching.

## Infrastructure

- **Cloud Platform From Zero:** Built an entire production AWS platform in Pulumi across isolated development, QA and production accounts.
- **Reusable Infrastructure Library:** Authored a shared Pulumi component library now powering cloud infrastructure across multiple company projects.

## Selection guidance

Strongest openers, in order: Halved Per-User AI Cost, Full-Stack Product Ownership, Real-Time AI Voice Coach, Shipped Paid Subscriptions.

Halved Per-User AI Cost leads because it is the only dollar figure in the entire folder. A reader converts $30 to $16 into money without knowing anything about the product.

Resting on scope rather than an outcome. Drop first: AI Personalisation Engine, Generative Audio Pipeline, Cloud Platform From Zero.

## Confirm before sending

- **$30 to $16 per user** is the strongest number available anywhere in these files. Know exactly how it was measured and over what period, because this is the one an interviewer will chase.
- **140 screens and 97 endpoints** need a counting method. Screens rendered, or route definitions? Endpoints, or controller methods?
- **"Multiple company projects"** for CloudForge should become a count once known. "Powering three projects" beats "multiple".
- **GPT-5** is named explicitly. Confirm it is still the model in use before sending, since a stale model name dates the resume.
- No user, revenue, retention, or download figures exist. Never add one.
