# Omnilocal

## Description
Omnilocal is an all-in-one platform that helps the real estate industry automate their business through their platform. It provides various integrations including but not limited to Google Business Profiles + Google Local Service Ads + Gmail + complex insights & visibility + FUB integration. Additionally, it has an automated scraper service that can be used to scrape the client's ad rankings in real-time.
