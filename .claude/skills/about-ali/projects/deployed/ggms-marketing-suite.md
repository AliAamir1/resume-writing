# Gallagher Group Marketing Suite (GGMS Plus)

## Description
GGMS is a digital marketing agency designed specifically for real estate agents. The company's mission is to help agents capture, nurture, and convert leads more effectively through innovative marketing tools and seamless CRM integrations.

With over 500,000 buyer and seller leads generated through Google and Facebook, GGMS has a proven track record of connecting agents with motivated clients. The platform also nurtures 6.3 million leads through its proprietary CRM system, keeping prospects engaged and ready for conversion.

Each month, GGMS identifies 180,000 hand-raisers — prospects who are actively seeking real estate services — using automated tools that streamline the lead-generation process. By combining real estate expertise with advanced digital marketing strategies, GGMS empowers agents to grow their businesses, convert more leads, and close more deals with ease.
