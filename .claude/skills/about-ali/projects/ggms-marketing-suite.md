# GGMS Marketing Suite

Marketing and CRM tooling for a digital marketing agency serving real-estate agents, covering lead capture, nurture, and conversion.

## Bullets

- *Lead Generation Platform*: Built marketing and CRM tooling for a real-estate marketing agency whose platform has generated over 500,000 buyer and seller leads and nurtures 6.3 million contacts through its proprietary CRM.
- *Marketing Automation*: Shipped automated tooling supporting identification of roughly 180,000 actively searching prospects per month out of the nurtured pool.
- *Web Scraping*: Built extensive scraping pipelines feeding the lead-generation process.
- *Containerized Deployment*: Deployed the MERN and TypeScript stack to AWS EC2 and ECR with Docker and GitHub Actions.
