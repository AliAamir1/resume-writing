# Omnilocal

All-in-one real-estate business automation platform with deep third-party integrations and a real-time ad-rank scraper.

## Bullets

- *Third-Party Platform Integration*: Integrated Google Business Profile, Google Local Service Ads, Gmail, and Follow Up Boss CRM into a single real-estate automation platform with unified insights and visibility reporting.
- *ETL and Web Scraping*: Built an automated scraper service tracking client advertising rankings in real time.
- *Serverless AWS Architecture*: Built on S3, Lambda, SQS, Amplify, and Cognito with Prisma over MySQL and Convex for real-time data.
- *Authentication*: Implemented authentication with Clerk and AWS Cognito across the platform.
