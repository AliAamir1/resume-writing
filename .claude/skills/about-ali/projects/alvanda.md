# Alvanda

B2B business process management platform unifying process, task, and team coordination with standardized repeatable workflows.

## Bullets

- *Real-Time Collaboration*: Built a business process management platform with live task and team coordination over Socket.IO backed by Redis.
- *Workflow Standardization*: Modeled repeatable standardized processes with centralized task delegation so operations produce predictable outcomes rather than ad-hoc handoffs.
- *MERN Stack Development*: Built on React with Redux, Express, Node.js, MongoDB, and TypeScript end to end.
